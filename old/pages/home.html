It’s nice to meet you

For over a decade, I’ve been championing product and UX design at Bay Area startups. I entered the world of product design early, receiving my bachelor’s degree in Human-Computer Interaction, and have devoted my entire career to shaping how people engage with technology 🤖

I’m the type of design leader who always stays connected to the work, and even though my title says Director, I prefer to call myself an experience designer ✨ My role goes far beyond crafting screens or managing people—it’s about orchestrating a journey for someone to take when they interact with my products. It’s about creating cohesion, meaning, and delight across the whole experience, and while I love the creativity of designing beautiful, functional interfaces in Figma (cue the “oohs” and “ahhs”), my favorite part of what I do is often behind the scenes 🫣

It’s in designing seamless workflows that often go unnoticed, building systems that empower product teams to communicate, fostering cultures where people thrive, and making products people want to use. It’s about bringing order to chaos, elegance to the everyday, and expression to what often goes unsaid.

And in the messy, complex, and intangible challenges of this field, I believe experience design has the power to make a true impact on the world 🌸
