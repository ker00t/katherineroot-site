But what about AI

How do I feel about AI?

Ah, the question of the hour. AI is incredible. It's allowing us all more freedom to express and explore our ideas in real time, and has the capacity to take a lot of burden out of our processes and workflows. In fact, I built this site using Claude Code. But it has its limitations, and there's still a lot of the creative process I prefer to do by hand. Whatever work you see from me, my ideas and fingerprints are still all over it, even if I used AI for help.
